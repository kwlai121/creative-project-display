Dr. Chen Salud Dental had operated on word-of-mouth referrals for 70 years, with zero digital presence. Every new patient's first digital touchpoint had to work — and had to work for everyone.

### Key Challenges

- **Real-time Requirements** — Costa Rican patients default to WhatsApp over email or contact forms. The site needed a contact system — WhatsApp, phone, and a form — where every message reliably reached the clinic's inbox immediately, with spam filtered out without blocking real patients.
- **Technical Accessibility** — As a healthcare touchpoint, the site had to work for patients navigating by keyboard or screen reader, not as an afterthought. That meant computing real WCAG contrast ratios, fixing semantic heading order, and verifying visible focus states, not just eyeballing it.
