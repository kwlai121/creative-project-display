The rebuild shipped ahead of the program's application window (August 3–18), the hard constraint the whole project was scoped around. There was no usable analytics baseline to compare against — the previous site wasn't meaningfully indexed by Google, so "before" means a template literally titled "Free HTML Templates."

What changed is concrete: the contact form works and reliably reaches the team's inbox instead of silently discarding messages. The site now ships real meta titles, descriptions, and schema.org structured data instead of placeholder text. And the whole experience — from CTA consistency to load time on a throttled connection — was built and tested against the mobile-first, budget-constrained reality of the people actually applying.
